Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $9.99. Orders over $50 qualify for free standard shipping.

Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship to over 40 countries. International delivery takes 7 to 14 business days. Customs duties and import taxes are paid by the customer.

How can I track my order?
After your order ships, we email you a tracking number. You can also see tracking details under "My Orders" in your account.

Can I change or cancel my order?
Orders can be changed or cancelled within 1 hour of being placed. After that, the order is sent to our warehouse and can no longer be modified; you can return it after delivery instead.

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. We do not accept cash on delivery.

Do you offer a warranty?
All electronics come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering defects. The warranty does not cover accidental damage or water damage.

What are your customer support hours?
Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern Time. Tickets created outside these hours are answered the next business day.

How do I reset my password?
Click "Forgot password" on the login page and enter your email address. You will receive a reset link valid for 30 minutes.
