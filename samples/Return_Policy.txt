Return and Refund Policy

Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include the receipt or order number.

Non-returnable items
Gift cards, downloadable software, personal care products, and items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned.

Electronics
Electronics such as phones, laptops and headphones can be returned within 15 days of delivery. Opened electronics are subject to a 10% restocking fee.

How to start a return
Log in to your account, go to "My Orders", select the item and click "Start a return". You will receive a prepaid return shipping label by email within 24 hours.

Refund timing
Once we receive your return, we inspect it within 3 business days. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method and usually appear within 5 to 7 business days.

Exchanges
We do not offer direct exchanges. Please return the original item for a refund and place a new order.

Damaged or wrong items
If your item arrived damaged or you received the wrong item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo and we will send a replacement at no cost.
